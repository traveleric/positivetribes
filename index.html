Current digital wellness project

PulseLift

PulseLift is an iPhone workout and wellness app being developed by Positive Tribes. It helps people organize exercise plans, track completed workouts, and maintain personal wellness records on their own device.

Private by designPersonal workout information stays on the user’s device. No advertising or third-party analytics.
Accessible and practicalClear tools help people plan workouts, record progress, and develop consistent routines.
Built for community benefitPractical technology supporting healthier, more connected lives.
