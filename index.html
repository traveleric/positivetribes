About us

Belonging is something we build together.

Positive Tribes exists to strengthen the everyday connections that help people and communities thrive. We bring a practical, human-centered approach to community support and wellness: listening first, making useful resources accessible, and creating tools that help people take positive action.

Current digital wellness project

PulseLift

PulseLift is an iPhone workout and wellness app being developed by Positive Tribes. It helps people organize exercise plans, track completed workouts, and maintain personal wellness records on their own device. The app is designed around simplicity, privacy, and everyday usefulness.

Private by designPersonal workout information stays on the user’s device. The app does not use advertising or third-party analytics.
Accessible and practicalClear tools help people plan workouts, record progress, and develop consistent routines.
Built for community benefitPulseLift reflects our broader mission: use practical technology to support healthier, more connected lives.
